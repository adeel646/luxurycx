Ref. 10 — Industry Insight

What we've learned building for this world.

01

A luxury buyer rarely messages a brand as a first step. By the time they reach out, they've already researched the piece for weeks — the reply they get next carries unusual weight.

02

Trust in this category is won or lost in tone before it's won or lost in price. A rushed or generic reply reads as a lower-tier retailer, regardless of what's in stock.

03

The channel a buyer chooses — WhatsApp, DM, or website — is rarely the channel they stay on. A Concierge that remembers the conversation across all three is doing what a good advisor already does in person.

Ref. 13 — Founder's Note

Why a watchmaker's precision, applied to customer experience.

Meridian was founded on a simple observation: luxury brands obsess over precision in their product, and improvise everywhere else. A house that will not tolerate a movement running a few seconds off will still let a buyer's message sit unanswered for a day.

Ref. 14 — Questions

Before you book a call.

Is this just a chatbot with a different name? +
No. A chatbot answers questions. A Concierge qualifies intent, matches tone to your brand, and knows when to bring in a human — which is most of the time, for your highest-value buyers.
What happens when a buyer wants a real person? +
The Concierge is built to recognize that moment and hand off immediately — with full context passed to your advisor, so the buyer never repeats themselves.
Do you only work with watch brands? +
Watches and fine jewelry are the focus, deliberately. The buying psychology, trust signals, and objection patterns in this category are specific — and that specialization is what makes the Concierge feel native to your business.
How long does deployment take? +
Most Concierge builds go from discovery call to live deployment within a few weeks, depending on how many channels and languages are involved.
Will it sound like my brand, or like a generic AI? +
Every Concierge is written from your existing materials and reviewed by your team before launch. Nothing goes live until it sounds like something you'd say yourself.
